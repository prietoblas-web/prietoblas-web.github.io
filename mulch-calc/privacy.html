Privacy Policy

Mulch Calculator — Blas Apps

Overview

Mulch Calculator is a mulch volume calculator. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device only. The app does not transmit any personal data to external servers. There are no user accounts, no cloud sync, and no remote databases.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — Displays advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchase — Processes payments through Apple's payment infrastructure, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Associates — The app may contain affiliate links governed by Amazon's Privacy Policy.

Personal Data

The developer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Any information you enter is stored locally on your device and is never sent to external servers.

Analytics

The app does not include any first-party analytics or tracking. The only analytics data that may be collected is through Google AdMob as part of ad delivery, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time from the app's Settings screen. Alternatively, uninstalling the app will remove all stored data from your device.

Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
